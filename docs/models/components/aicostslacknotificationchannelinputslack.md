| `WebhookURL`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | `string`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | :heavy_check_mark:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | The Slack incoming webhook URL that receives the channel's messages. The organization creates the webhook in its own Slack workspace.<br/><br/>The URL has the form `https://hooks.slack.com/services/<workspace ID>/<webhook ID>/<token>`. Each of the 3 parts is nonempty and contains only ASCII letters, digits, `_`, or `-`. It has no credentials, port, query, or fragment and is at most 2048 characters long. Slack workflow trigger URLs, under `/workflows/` or `/triggers/`, aren't accepted.<br/><br/>The service never logs the URL. Reads return it with `<token>` replaced by `…`. |
